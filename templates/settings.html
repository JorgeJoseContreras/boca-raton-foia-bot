Dynamic Placeholders:
  • {addressee}: Dynamically evaluated to the specific custodian title and municipality (e.g., Town Clerk / Records Custodian of Town of Hillsboro Beach or City Clerk / Records Custodian of City of Boca Raton).
  • {date_of_request} or {current_date}: Dynamically evaluated to the live date of dispatch (e.g., August 17, 2026).
  • {start_date}: Evaluated to the demolition search start date (e.g., January 1, 2024).
  • {city_name}: Dynamically evaluated to the specific recipient municipality.
